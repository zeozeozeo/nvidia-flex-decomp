# 028 `cs_5_0` — SolveShapes (NVAPI variant)

**Status: blocked (not reconstructed) — toolchain limit.**

## Interface
- no constant buffer
- SRV: `rigidOffsets` (t0), `rigidIndices` (t1), `rigidCoefficients` (t2), `reverseLookup` (t5),
  `localNormals` (t7, stride 16) — all stride 4 except t7
- UAV: `u0` raw (`InterlockedFloat::accum`), `u1` stride 12 (`localPositions`), `u2`/`u3`/`u5`
  stride 16 (`newPositions`, `rotations`, `normals`), `u4` stride 12 (`translations`),
  **`dcl_uav_structured_opc u7, 256`** (`g_NvidiaExt`)
- thread group 64x1x1; inputs `SV_GroupID` + `SV_GroupThreadID`; `groupshared` several blocks

## Algorithm
Shape (rigid) contact resolution for one particle per thread group member with the accumulated
position stored in the raw UAV, plus per-contact emission through the NVAPI extension: 269
references to `u7`, 128 `imm_atomic_alloc` and 151 `store_structured`, alongside 24 `store_raw`
and 21 `ld_raw` on the raw UAV.

## Blocker (hard, toolchain)
`dcl_uav_structured_opc` is not expressible in this front end (see 027). Two further limits
would apply even if it were: the body reads the raw UAV with `ld_raw`, which
`vkd3d-shader 2.1` cannot emit either. Recorded as a toolchain limit, not a reconstruction gap.
