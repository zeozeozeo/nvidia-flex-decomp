# 047 `cs_5_0` — TransformShapeBounds

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` `cb0[1]` immediateIndexed (`int gNumBounds`)
- SRV `t0`/`t1`/`t2`/`t3` stride 16 (`positions`, `rotations`, `positionsPrev`,
  `rotationsPrev`), `t4` 4 (`flags`), `t5` 16 (`geometry`), `t6`/`t7` 48 (`convexMeshes`,
  `triangleMeshes`)
- UAV `u0`/`u1` stride 16 (`lowers`, `uppers`)
- thread group 256×1×1

## Algorithm
For each bound: shape type = `flags[i] & 7`, local half extents from `geometry[i]` (read with a
`.yzxw` swizzle in the bytecode): sphere `r`, capsule `(r, r, r + halfHeight)`, box
`(g.y, g.z, g.x)`, convex/triangle mesh scaled by the mesh's padded lower/upper bounds (indices
`geometry.w - 1`), SDF `(0, r)`. The centre `(lo + hi)/2` is rotated by the quaternion and
offset by `positions[i]`; the world half extent is `|R| * (hi - lo) * 0.5`. The written lower
and upper bounds are the union over the current and previous transforms (swept bounds), with
`w = 0`.

## Inferences
- The bytecode's matrix construction is the fxc expansion of a quaternion rotation matrix; the
  helper uses the standard symmetric form. `Rotate()` is transcribed exactly
  (`v*(w*w-1) + 2*q.xyz*dot + 2*w*cross(v,q)`), including the non-unit-quaternion `w*w-1`
  coefficient.
- `convexMeshes`/`triangleMeshes` stride 48 B match the RDEF member list: lower(12) + plane
  offset/index start(4) + upper(12) + counts(4) + padding(16).
- Shape types 6/7 fall through `default`, leaving the extents at their pre-switch values (the
  original also reads an uninitialised register there).
