# 027 `cs_5_0` — CalculateBounds (NVAPI variant)

**Status: blocked (not reconstructed) — toolchain limit.**

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 24 registers)
- `StructuredBuffer<float4> readBoundsLower` (t2, stride 16), `readBoundsUpper` (t3, stride 16)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> writeBoundsLower` (u0, stride 16), `writeBoundsUpper` (u1, stride 16)
- **`dcl_uav_structured_opc u7, 256`** (`g_NvidiaExt`, `NvShaderExtnStruct`)
- `groupshared float3` 4 entries; thread group 128x1x1; inputs `SV_GroupID`, `SV_GroupThreadID`, `SV_DispatchThreadID`

## Algorithm
The NVAPI analogue of 024/039: 121 references to `u7`, 60 of them `imm_atomic_alloc` (NVAPI
append allocation), the rest `store_structured` into the allocated slot. The min/max reduction
of the lower and upper element bounds therefore runs through the NVAPI extension buffer rather
than shared memory, and the bounds pair is written to u0/u1.

## Blocker (hard, toolchain)
`dcl_uav_structured_opc` cannot be emitted by `vkd3d-shader 2.1`: `AppendStructuredBuffer` /
`ConsumeStructuredBuffer` are rejected by the HLSL front end (`E5030`), `.Append()` /
`.IncrementCounter()` are undefined on `RWStructuredBuffer` (`E5005`), and a plain
`RWStructuredBuffer` emits `dcl_uav_structured u7, 256` without `_opc`. Any HLSL spelling fails
the binding check on that one declaration. Recorded as a toolchain limit, not a reconstruction
gap. Same blocker as 028/031/045/046/048/049/050/051/054.
