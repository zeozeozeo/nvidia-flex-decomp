# 076 `cs_5_0` — CollideTriangles

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 32 registers) + `cbuffer constSubstep` (b1, 1 register)
- `StructuredBuffer`: t0 (16), t1 (4), t2 (16), t3 (16), t4 (4), t5 (16), t6 (16), t7 (4),
  t8 (16), t9 (16), t10 (4), t11 (16), t12 (16), t13 (16), t14 (4), t15 (16), t16 (16), t17 (48)
- `RWStructuredBuffer` u0 (4), u1 (16), u2 (16); thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
Per particle: swept bound from `sortedPositions`/`sortedNewPositions` expanded by
`kCollisionMargin`; traverse the shape BVH (`shapeBvhRootNode`, `shapeBvhNodeLowers/Uppers`,
leaf flag in the sign bit of `lower.w`), skip non-mesh shapes (`flags & 7 != 4`) and phase-masked
shapes, transform to mesh space with the substep-interpolated transform, then traverse the mesh's
triangle BVH (`bvhRootNodeArray`/`bvhNodeLowersArray`/`bvhNodeUppersArray`, offsets from
`meshOffsets`), compute the closest point on each candidate triangle (clamped barycentrics) and
emit a contact plane/velocity pair for every hit closer than `kCollisionDistance`, bounded by
`kMaxContactsPerParticle`. `collisionCounts` receives the total.

## Inference (recorded, not exact)
- The assembly keeps two 40-entry local stacks plus a 96-byte-per-lane groupshared triangle cache
  (`dcl_indexableTemp x0/x1/x2[40]`, `g1, 96, 256`); the reconstruction uses plain local stacks
  and no shared cache. Traversal order is therefore the same, the caching strategy is not.
- `cb1[0]` is reconstructed as `float4 gSubParams`; its two used components are the substep
  interpolation fractions (`mad ... cb1[0].x`, `mad ... cb1[0].y`).
- The shape bounding-sphere rejection uses `shapeGeometry` (t13), which the shipped bytecode also
  reads; the exact component meaning is inferred.
