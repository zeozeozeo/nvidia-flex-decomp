# 071 `cs_5_0` — SpringsReorder

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 1 register) with `int gNumSprings`
- `Buffer<uint> sortedIndices` (t0), `StructuredBuffer<int> indices` (t1, 4),
  `StructuredBuffer<float> lengths` (t2, 4), `stiffness` (t3, 4)
- `RWStructuredBuffer` u0/u1/u2 (stride 4); thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
`key = sortedIndices[i]`; `spring = key >> 1`, `parity = key & 1`; the opposite particle is
`key - 1` for odd keys and `key + 1` for even keys. The three outputs are gathered with that
index (indices) and with `spring` (lengths, stiffness).

## Inference
- `gNumSprings * 2` is the dispatch bound (`ishl r0.x, cb0[0].x, 1`).
- Element types of t2/t3 are `float` (stride 4 is identical for int/float).
