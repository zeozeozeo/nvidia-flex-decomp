# 015 `cs_5_0` — CompactDiffuseParticles

## Interface
- `StructuredBuffer<int> numDiffuseParticles` (t0, stride 4)
- `StructuredBuffer<float4> diffusePositions` (t1), `diffuseVelocities` (t2)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<uint> numDiffuseParticlesNew` (u0, stride 4)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> diffusePositionsNew` (u1), `diffuseVelocitiesNew` (u2)
- thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
For each of the `numDiffuseParticles[0]` live particles with `position.w > 0`, append into the
new buffers at `InterlockedAdd(numDiffuseParticlesNew[0], 1)` — a stable compaction of the
diffuse particle set.

## Inference
- The `w` lane of the diffuse position is the lifetime; `w > 0` marks a live particle.
