# 019 `cs_5_0` — ClearCellBuckets

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 25 registers)
- `StructuredBuffer<int> activeIndices` (t0, stride 4), `StructuredBuffer<float4> newPositions` (t1, stride 16),
  `StructuredBuffer<float3> gBounds` (t2, stride 12)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<int> cellBucketStarts` (u0, stride 4), `cellBucketEnds` (u1, stride 4)
- thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
For each active particle: compute its cell coordinate from the grid lower bound and
`kInvCellEdge`, truncate, clamp into `[0, 511]`, fold into the 21-bit grid hash
`(x & 127) | (y & 127) << 7 | (z & 127) << 14`, and zero both bucket counters at that hash.

## Inference
- The hash shape matches the CUDA `CreateCellIndices` (`flex.cu:725`), which uses
  `(cz << 14) | (cy << 7) | cx` with `& 127` per axis; the 512 clamp is the D3D variant's
  `kInvCellEdge` normalisation. The 7-bit folding therefore aliases cells whose
  coordinate exceeds 127, which is what the original bytecode does.
- `bfi`/`iadd` were folded into the single OR expression; the bit layout is identical.
