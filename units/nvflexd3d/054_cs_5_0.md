# 054 `cs_5_0` — SolveInflatableVolume (not reconstructed)

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` `cb0[25]` immediateIndexed (through `kSubStepLength..kStaticTriGridInvCellEdge`)
- SRV: `t0`/`t1` structured stride 16 (inflatables positions), `t2`/`t3`/`t4` stride 4
  (`reverseLookup`, `indices`, `lambdas`)
- UAV: `u0` raw (`InterlockedFloat::accum`), **`dcl_uav_structured_opc u7, 256`**
- groupshared: `g0` 16 B; thread group 512×1×1
- entry `SV_GroupID` + `SV_GroupThreadID`

## Algorithm
Computes the volume constraint per inflatable (512-thread reduction into `g0`), accumulates
the correction into `u0` with float atomics and emits NVAPI extension commands on `u7`
(opcode `13` at offsets 0/76/92/108/140); 64 `u7` references.

## Blocker (hard, toolchain)
`dcl_uav_structured_opc` not emittable from `vkd3d-shader 2.1` HLSL (`E5030`/`E5005`).
Documented, not attempted.
