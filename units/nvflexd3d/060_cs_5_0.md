# 060 `cs_5_0` — NormalizeVertexNormals

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 24 registers)
- `Buffer<uint> indices` (t0), `ByteAddressBuffer tempNormals` (t1, `dcl_resource_raw`)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> normals` (u0, stride 16)
- thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
For every particle: `n = tempNormals[index]` (3 floats at `index * 16`), normalise it and
store `float4(-n, 0)` at `normals[index]`. A zero-length normal maps to zero because the
`rsqrt` result is masked by the `0 < dot(n,n)` predicate.

## Inference
- The negation is explicit in the asm (`mov r1.xyz, -r0.yzwy`) and is kept: the stored
  normal has the opposite sign of the accumulated one.
