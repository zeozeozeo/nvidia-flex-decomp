# 045 `cs_5_0` — SolveShapes (not reconstructed)

## Interface (from `work/dxbc/NvFlexReleaseD3D_x64/045_cs_5_0.asm` / `work/dxbc/interfaces.txt`)
- no constant buffer
- SRV: `t0..t5` structured stride 4 (`rigidOffsets`, `rigidIndices`, `rigidCoefficients`,
  `rigidPlasticThresholds`, `rigidPlasticCreeps`, `reverseLookup`), `t6`/`t7` structured stride 16
  (`oldPositions`, `localNormals`)
- UAV: `u0` raw (`InterlockedFloat::accum`), `u1` stride 12 (`localPositions`), `u2`/`u3`/`u5`
  stride 16 (`newPositions`, `rotations`, `normals`), `u4` stride 12 (`translations`),
  **`dcl_uav_structured_opc u7, 256`** (`g_NvidiaExt`, `NvShaderExtnStruct`)
- groupshared: `g0`/`g1` 12 B, `g2` 36 B, `g3`/`g4` 4 B, `g5` 12×64, `g6` 16 B
- thread group 64×1×1; entry `SV_GroupID` (shape) + `SV_GroupThreadID`

## Algorithm
Same three-pass structure as 042 (average particle position → covariance → rotation
extraction → apply delta), but every contact is additionally emitted through the NVAPI
extension UAV `u7` with `imm_atomic_alloc` + `store_structured` (opcode `3` payload at
offsets 0/76). 269 references to `u7`.

## Blocker (hard, toolchain)
`dcl_uav_structured_opc` (structured UAV with an operator counter) cannot be emitted by
`vkd3d-shader 2.1`: `AppendStructuredBuffer` / `ConsumeStructuredBuffer` are rejected by the
HLSL front end (`E5030: Unknown modifier "AppendStructuredBuffer"`), `.Append()` /
`.IncrementCounter()` are undefined on `RWStructuredBuffer` (`E5005`), and a plain
`RWStructuredBuffer` emits `dcl_uav_structured u7, 256` without `_opc`. Any HLSL spelling
therefore fails the binding check on that one declaration. Recorded as a toolchain limit, not
a reconstruction gap.
