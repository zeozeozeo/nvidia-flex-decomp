# 061 `cs_5_0` — UpdateDiffuseParticles (not reconstructed)

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` **`cb0[32], dynamicIndexed`** (full `KernelParams` through `kMaxContactsPerParticle`)
- SRV: `t0` stride 4 (`numDiffuseParticles`), `t1`/`t2` stride 4 (`cellBucketStarts`,
  `cellBucketEnds`), `t3` stride 4 (`contactCounts`), `t4`/`t5` stride 16
  (`sortedNewPositionsTex`, `sortedNewVelocitiesTex`), `t7` stride 12 (`gBounds`)
- UAV: `u0` stride 16 (`diffusePositions`), `u1` stride 16 (`diffuseVelocities`)
- thread group 256×1×1; entry `SV_DispatchThreadID`

## Algorithm (transcribed from the bytecode; the HLSL below is NOT committed because the
## interface cannot match)
Cell lookup of the diffuse particle, neighbourhood average velocity over the bucket
(`sumVel/sumWeight`, density from `contactCounts`), buoyancy/drag blending, a ballistic
integration variant selected by `kDiffuseBallistic`, per-velocity clamping against
`kDiffuseMaxVelocity`, then a loop over the `kNumPlanes` static planes (`kPlanes[i]`,
collision distance `kCollisionDistance`, restitution factor 1.5) and a lifetime decrement
`dp.w - kDiffuseDt`.

## Blocker (hard, toolchain)
The declaration is `dcl_constantBuffer cb0[32], dynamicIndexed`, but `vkd3d-shader 2.1`
always emits `immediateIndexed`: probed with (a) a loop counter index into `kPlanes`,
(b) `tid.x & 7`, (c) an index loaded from a resource buffer (that one even lowers to
`cb0[r0.x + 0]` addressing) — all three still dump `dcl_constantBuffer cb0[N], immediateIndexed`.
The flag is part of the hard-checked binding string, so the shader cannot pass; recorded as a
toolchain limit.
