# 014 `cs_5_0` — ReorderParticles

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 24 registers)
- `StructuredBuffer<float4> positions` (t0), `newPositions` (t1), `StructuredBuffer<float3> velocities` (t2),
  `StructuredBuffer<int> phases` (t3), `Buffer<uint> indices` (t4), `StructuredBuffer<float4> normals` (t5)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> sortedPositions` (u0), `sortedNewPositions` (u1), `sortedVelocities` (u2),
  `RWStructuredBuffer<int> sortedPhases` (u3), `RWStructuredBuffer<uint> reverseLookup` (u4),
  `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> sortedNormals` (u5)
- thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
For every particle: `index = indices[tid]`, scatter position/newPosition/phase/normal into the
sorted slots and `velocities[index]` (3 floats) into the xyz of the 16-byte sorted velocity,
then `reverseLookup[index] = tid`.

## Inference
- `sortedVelocities` has stride 16 while the source `velocities` has stride 12, so w is
  preserved with a read-modify-write exactly as the bytecode's `store_structured u2.xyz`.
