# 020 `cs_5_0` — SmoothPositions

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 24 registers)
- `StructuredBuffer<int> contacts` (t0), `contactCounts` (t1), `phases` (t2)
- `StructuredBuffer<float4> sortedNewPositionsTex` (t3), `Buffer<uint> indices` (t4)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> smoothPositions` (u0), `smoothPositionsOriginal` (u1)
- thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
Self-colliding particles (`phase & (1 << 22)`) accumulate, over their contact list, the
Polynomial-kernel weighted neighbour position and the sum of the kernel weights `w`.
The average is `sum.xyz / sumW`, the blend weight is `min(sumW / ((1 - h^3) * 4), 1) * kSmoothing`
with `h = 0.5 * kInvRadius * kRadius`, and the result is
`pos + (avg - pos) * blend`. Non-filtered particles are copied unchanged. The result is written
to `smoothPositions[tid]` and `smoothPositionsOriginal[index]`.

## Inference
- The kernel is `1 - q^3` with `q = |d| * kInvRadius` (`mul`/`mad` chain), the polynomial
  smoothing kernel of the CUDA `SmoothPositions`.
- `kNumParticlesAligned` is the neighbourhood-list column stride added to the index walker.
