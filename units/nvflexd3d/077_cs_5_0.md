# 077 `cs_5_0` — SolveVelocities

## Interface
- `cbuffer consts` (b0, 32 registers)
- `StructuredBuffer`: t0 (4), t1 (4), t2 (16), t3 (4), t4 (16), t5 (16), t6 (16), t7 (4), t8 (16)
- `RWStructuredBuffer<float4> sortedNewPositions` (u0, 16), `RWByteAddressBuffer deltas` (u1),
  `RWStructuredBuffer<float> densities` (u2, 4), `potentials` (u3, 4)
- thread group 256x1x1

## Algorithm
Viscosity (`w^2 * kSpiky1 * kFreeSurfaceDrag * dt * (vj - vi)`), vorticity accumulation and the
cohesion/surface-tension term are accumulated over the contact list for diffuse-diffuse pairs;
the vorticity confinement force is `cross(curl, w) * dt^2 * kInvRestDensity *
kVorticityConfinement`; dissipation and damping rescale the delta; restitution resolves collisions
against `collisionPlanes` (`tid * kMaxContactsPerParticle` stride); particles below
`kSleepThresholdSq` are restored from `sortedPositions`. `deltas` is written raw, `densities`
receives the neighbour weight sum, `potentials` the cohesion potential.

## Inference (recorded, not exact)
- The bytecode's register permutation (`mov r4.xyzw, r4.yzzx`) before the curl cross product is a
  component shuffle; the reconstruction uses the unshuffled vectors.
- `cb0[15].x` (`kDiffuseThreshold`, offset 60) gates the cohesion block; that mapping is taken
  from the RDEF variable table, not from the algorithm's semantics.
- The potentials store (`u3`) is only reachable when that gate is non-zero, matching the
  bytecode's `if_nz r2.z` structure.
